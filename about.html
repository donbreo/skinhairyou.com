WELCOME TO SKIN HAIR & YOU

Beyond treatment.
Genuine care.

Established in the year 2012, Skin, Hair & You is a recognised clinic for skin care, cosmetic and hair services. We specialise in a range of result-driven aesthetic treatments, with personalised care for different ages and skin types.

Our key areas of expertise include dermatology, hair transplant, cosmetology, anti-ageing and laser. Our services are provided in a professional, service-oriented environment, backed by ethical practices, modern technology and certified experts.

With clinics in Mumbai and Pune, we offer personalised treatments for better skin and hair. We understand that the desire to feel comfortable and confident in your own skin is personal, irrespective of age or gender.
