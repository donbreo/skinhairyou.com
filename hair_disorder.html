Our services

Hair Disorders.

Understand your hair loss and explore a restoration plan that is right for you.

Care from root to tip

A healthier relationship with your hair starts here.

SK&U offers natural hair restoration treatments for male and female pattern hair loss. Our range of services includes mesotherapy, unwanted hair reduction by laser, dermatoscopic analysis and platelet rich plasma (PRP).

Hair loss conditions can affect your scalp, self-esteem and confidence. The right choice of therapy and treatment is based on the type of hair disorder, your age, gender and the extent of the condition.

Whether you are experiencing an autoimmune disorder like alopecia areata or hair loss associated with thyroid or other medical conditions, our experts can evaluate your concerns and discuss appropriate treatment options.

We take the time to understand your hair and scalp, explain your options and recommend a personalised approach. The response to treatment varies, and a consultation is the first step.

This information is general and does not replace a consultation. Treatment suitability and outcomes vary. Our team can help you understand your individual options.
