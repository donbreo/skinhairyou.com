Facial Cleansing.

Refresh your routine with cleansing and hydration suited to your skin.

A fresh start for your skin

Simple care. A fresh, healthy feeling.

A common benefit of facial cleansing is the removal of dirt, oil and other unwanted debris. Throughout the day, the skin is exposed to bacteria, pollutants, dirt and dead skin cells. Gentle daily washing removes these impurities and leaves the skin feeling fresh.

Regular facial cleansing, along with a suitable moisturiser, helps the skin maintain hydration. Dehydrated skin can look and feel rough. A gentle, pH-balanced routine supports the skin barrier without stripping away its natural oils.

Why regular cleansing matters

Without regular cleansing, oil and debris may build up and contribute to clogged pores or irritation. Avoid harsh scrubbing or overwashing, which can also irritate skin. A consistent, gentle routine helps keep skin comfortable.

Other benefits of cleansing

Cleansing removes surface impurities, prepares skin for other skincare products and supports a routine that balances hydration and oil. It is an important starting point for healthy-looking skin.

How to choose the right facial cleanser

Know your skin type and choose a cleanser that is a good match. For dry skin, avoid high-alcohol cleansers and consider a cream-based option. Gel or foaming cleansers may suit oily skin, while sensitive skin often benefits from a gentle, fragrance-free formula. Choose a balanced pH and avoid products that leave your skin feeling tight or irritated.
