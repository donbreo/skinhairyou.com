Nail Disorders.

Expert assessment and treatment for stronger, healthier-looking nails.

Small details. Complete care.

Give your nails the attention they deserve.

Some of the most commonly occurring nail disorders include fungal infections, warts and ingrown nails. Conditions like psoriasis or lichen planus may also affect the nails and cause brittleness.

Excessive nail polish, manicures, pedicures and cosmetics can damage sensitive nails. Changes in nail colour, texture or strength are worth having assessed.

Our professionals first examine your nails, determine the extent of any infection and recommend an appropriate course of treatment. We also offer nail avulsion surgery when clinically appropriate for severe or recurring nail problems.

This information is general and does not replace a consultation. Treatment suitability and outcomes vary. Our team can help you understand your individual options.
